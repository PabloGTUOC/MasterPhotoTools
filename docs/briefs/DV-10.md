## Brief

### Why
The owner wants photographs that look finished straight out of Develop. Demosaicing and the anti-aliasing
filter leave a RAW slightly soft. Capture sharpening restores what the optics and sensor blurred, before
any creative step.

### Method
**Richardson–Lucy deconvolution with a Gaussian blur model, on linear luminance.**
- **Luminance:** ProPhoto Y. Each pixel's RGB is scaled by its luminance gain, so hue and saturation do not
  change, and colour noise and fringes are not sharpened.
- **Iterations:** 10. Each one multiplies the estimate by the blurred ratio of the original to the blurred
  estimate.
- **Radius:**
  - σ = 0.7 px for Bayer sensors and 0.85 px for X-Trans, which demosaics a little softer;
  - fixed by sensor type, not measured from the picture. RawTherapee estimates the radius per photograph;
    that is left for later, and the report says so.
- **No halos:** the result is clamped to the 3×3 range of the original luminance. Sharpening cannot
  overshoot an edge, by construction.
- **No sharpened noise:** a contrast mask fades sharpening out where local contrast is low, so flat sky and
  skin keep the noise reduction's smoothness.
- **Strength:** 0–100 blends between the original and the deconvolved result.

**Where it runs:** after lens correction and noise reduction, before the look. Film grain is added after
it, so grain is never sharpened.

**Preview:** not sharpened. The preview is a reduced image, where a 0.7 px correction is invisible, so
sharpening it would only cost time. Capture sharpening shows at 100 % in the developed file.

### Build
- `media::develop::sharpen::capture_sharpen(width, height, pixels, amount, sigma)`.
- **`capture_sharpening: f32`**, 0–100:
  - on `DevelopItem` (serde default 0, refused outside 0–100) and `DevelopAction` (skipped when 0, so
    plans are unchanged);
  - on `DevelopRequest`, CLI `--sharpen <0..100>`, MCP `capture_sharpening`.
- **Screen:** a "Capture sharpening" slider, default 50 for a new photograph, carried into the dry run.
  Changing it locks Run.

### Acceptance
1. **An edge** blurred with a Gaussian of σ 0.7 comes back with its 10–90 % width at least **25 % narrower**
   at strength 100, and nothing goes outside the original edge's range.
2. **A flat field with noise** keeps its standard deviation within **5 %** at strength 100 (the mask).
3. **Strength 0** returns the input unchanged, and the RGB ratios of every pixel are kept at any strength.
4. **Plans:** a plan without sharpening serialises as before; a value outside 0–100 is skipped with its
   reason.
5. **Develop:** sharpening runs in the pipeline, and a sharpened output differs from an unsharpened one.
6. **Measured:** the time added per photograph on the owner's XZ-1 files.
7. **`check:develop` 31:** the slider defaults to 50, its value reaches the dry run, and a change locks Run.
8. **All gates pass.**

## Report

Implemented by Claude on 2026-11-14.

**What was done**
- **`media::develop::sharpen`:** `capture_sharpen` and `sigma_for`, built exactly as in the brief:
  - 10 Richardson–Lucy iterations on ProPhoto luminance, with a separable Gaussian;
  - the result clamped to the original's 3×3 range;
  - a contrast mask, 0 below 0.5 % relative contrast and 1 above 2.5 %;
  - RGB scaled by the luminance gain.
- **In `develop_one`:** after lens correction and noise reduction, before the look.
- **`capture_sharpening`:**
  - on `DevelopItem`, refused outside 0–100;
  - on `DevelopAction`, skipped when 0;
  - on `DevelopRequest`, CLI `--sharpen` and MCP `capture_sharpening`.
- **Screen:** a "Capture sharpening" slider, 50 for a new photograph.
- **Benchmark:** a strength argument.

**Deviation:** the radius is **fixed per sensor type** (0.7 px Bayer, 0.85 px X-Trans), not estimated from
each photograph as RawTherapee does. Estimating it is a step of its own, if the fixed radius proves wrong
on the owner's files (MV-45).

**Measured**
- **Synthetic edge** blurred with σ 0.7: 10–90 % rise **2.24 px → 1.43 px, 36 % narrower** at strength 100,
  with no value outside the original's range.
- **Owner's six XZ-1 files**, neutral JPEG with lens correction, one at a time, release build:

  With four photographs at once (DV-8), part of that overlaps with other photographs' serial steps.

**Mechanical test changes:** `capture_sharpening: 0.0` added to the existing `DevelopItem`,
