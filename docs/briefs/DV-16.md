# `DV-16` · The LX100's black level

> **Status:** approved
> **Plan:** [`docs/rescope-plan.md`](../rescope-plan.md), Develop. **Branch:** `dv-16`. A small fix made by the
> architect (the owner's standing rule for small fixes), so this file records the finding, the change and the
> check in one place, not three hands.

---

## Brief

### Why

The owner on 2026-11-23, after DV-15, about `P1100539.RW2`: *"the raw and the neutral develop look nothing
like one another"*. The "raw" in Develop's grid is the camera's embedded JPEG. The neutral had a **magenta
cast**. On grey paving, which the camera renders neutral, Develop's red/green was 1.064–1.087 on four of the
five files, and blue/green was up by 0.03–0.05.

The cause is the black level. rawler takes the LX100's black from IFD0 tags `0x1C`–`0x1E` (red, green,
blue), which say **128**. But in an ISO 400 frame not one pixel in ten thousand reads below 143–146. The
sensor's black is **143**: the file understates it by **15**. rawler's own LX100 profile carries a
commented-out `#blackpoint = 143`, and rawspeed (darktable's decoder) adds the same 15 to every Panasonic
body. Fifteen units of black left in every channel become a cast once the camera's white balance multiplies
red by 2.8 and blue by 1.5.

The architect confirmed it before changing any code: setting the three tags to 143 in a copy of the file
brought red/green from 1.087 to 1.011 (the camera's JPEG: 1.000).

rawler also lays these three values out as red, green, green, blue whatever the CFA, so on the LX100's BGGR
sensor the blue pixels would get red's value. It makes no difference on the LX100, whose three tags are
equal, but it would with uneven tags, and the fix places them correctly.

### What changed

`crates/rollchrome-core/src/media/develop/panasonic.rs`:
- `RW2_BLACK_BIAS = 15`, with the evidence in its comment.
- `rw2_black_levels(raw) -> Option<[u16; 3]>`: IFD0 `0x1C`–`0x1E` plus the bias. It returns `None`
  unless the header is `IIU\0` and all three tags are SHORTs.
- `black_by_cfa_position(rgb, cfa) -> [u16; 4]`: the levels laid out by the sensor's own 2×2 CFA, row by
  row, which is the order rawler's scaling reads them in.

`media/develop/mod.rs`, `develop_linear`: for a Bayer RW2, the decoded image's black level is replaced with
these levels before rawler scales the sensor values. Every other file develops exactly as before. The
preview, the export and every look go through `develop_linear`, so all of them get it.

Two tests: `the_rw2_black_level_carries_panasonic_s_bias` (128 → 143; uneven tags; a TIFF header, a
missing tag and a LONG are refused) and `the_black_level_follows_the_sensor_s_cfa` (BGGR and RGGB).

**Not addressed here, and reported to the owner:**
- **Brightness.** Develop's neutral is darker than the camera's JPEG: mean 0.37 against 0.43 on P1100539.
  DV-12 calibrated the neutral against Photomator per camera, and the LX100 has no calibration yet. Fitting
  one needs Photomator exports of these files.
- **Ektar's 8.2 MB, "loses resolution".** The owner's Ektar export is full size, 4272×2856, like the
  neutral. The full film simulation renders grain and halation, which soften fine detail as film does, and
  grain is noise that JPEG cannot compress: 8.2 MB against 2.2 MB for the neutral. Grain Off should make
  it much smaller (not measured). This is the simulation, not a fault.
- The owner's neutral export was about 0.3 EV brighter than Develop's default, presumably the exposure
  slider. It was not investigated.
